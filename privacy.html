Vettri Panchagam — Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · applies to the iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android apps and the website

Vettri Panchagam is a Tamil calendar and panchangam app. We built it so that nothing about you leaves your phone.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else.

Location

If you tap "Use my location", the app asks for permission to read your approximate location. It uses it only on your phone to calculate sunrise, sunset and Rahu Kalam times. To show your town's name, your phone's location service may look up the place name for those coordinates: on iPhone this is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and on Android by your phone's geocoding service (usually Google, under Google's Privacy Policy). You can skip this and choose a city instead, and you can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app checks each day's panchangam on your phone and shows a notification on the days you picked. Nothing is sent to a server.

Data stored on your phone

The app saves your chosen language and place on your phone so the app and its home-screen widget show the same settings. Deleting the app removes them.

The website

The website has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

If you allow it, your browser shares your location so the times are right for where you are. It is rounded to about 1 km and kept in a cookie in your browser, which is sent to our server with each page so it can calculate that day's panchangam. The server works out the nearest town's name from its own copy of the GeoNames list, so your location is never sent to anyone else, and it is not stored on the server or written to its logs. If you decline, only your time zone is used, and you can always pick a city instead.

The website's other cookies only remember your language, place and panchangam (Vakya or Thirukanitham). They are not used to identify or track you. Like any website, our server sees your IP address while it answers a request; we do not keep request logs.

The website runs on a server rented from Hetzner Online in the European Union.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
