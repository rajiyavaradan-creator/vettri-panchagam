Vettri Panchagam

A clean daily Tamil calendar with a complete panchangam, in Tamil or English: Tamil date, tithi, nakshatram, yogam, karanam, Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, Kuligai, hora, festivals and reminders, with home-screen widgets.

Available for Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Support

Questions, corrections or feedback: rajiyavaradan@gmail.com

Calculations use the Thirukanitham method with Lahiri ayanamsa. Calendars that follow the Vakya panchangam may differ by a day for some Tamil month starts.
